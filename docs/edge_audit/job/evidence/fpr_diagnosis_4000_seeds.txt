Reading: the plug-in SE sqrt((1 - g3*SR + (g4-1)/4*SR^2)/(T-1)) uses the sample skewness g3,
which co-moves with the sample SR under fat tails; when SR is high by luck, g3 is high, the SE
shrinks and the test fires too often. The null distribution of SR under H0 (SR = 0) has variance
1/(T-1) whatever g3 and g4 are, so the verdict now uses SE0 = 1/sqrt(T-1).
