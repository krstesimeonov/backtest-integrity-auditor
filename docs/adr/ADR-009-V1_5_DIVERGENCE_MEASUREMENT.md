# ADR-009: Measure divergence before interpreting it

Status: accepted for V1.5G.

## Decision

Project exact V1.5F Decimal differences into transaction-cost, sizing,
accounting, and return families through a canonical, hashable field policy.
Retain impaired evidence as counts and never aggregate heterogeneous units.

## Consequences

Every measurement is reproducible and traceable through normalization,
comparison, and measurement policy hashes. Later attribution can reason from
stable evidence, but V1.5G itself cannot claim cause, severity, or acceptability.
