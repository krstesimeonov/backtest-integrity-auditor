# V1.5F Deterministic Alignment and Comparison

Status: implemented in package `1.5.0a8`.

V1.5F consumes V1.5E `NormalizedReconciliation` evidence and produces exact,
typed differences for every requested comparison level. It does not decide
whether a difference is acceptable and does not infer a cause.

## Alignment contract

Each requested level has exactly one explicit policy. Semantic alignment uses
canonical domain keys such as timestamp, instrument, side, currency, or return
period. Source-ID alignment is available only where the canonical record has a
source identifier. Cash balances therefore remain semantically aligned even
when cash movements use source IDs.

Duplicate keys are reported as `ambiguous`; records are never paired by tuple
position or an arbitrary tie-break. One-sided records are retained as
`reference_only` or `candidate_only`.

## Evidence states

- `compared` means both engines supplied the level, including available-empty.
- `reference_unavailable`, `candidate_unavailable`, and `both_unavailable`
  preserve missing evidence without converting it into an empty collection.
- Matched records are `matched_equal` or `matched_different`.
- Differences contain canonical typed values plus exact Decimal deltas or
  integer microsecond deltas where applicable.

Engine-local IDs and lineage remain in the serialized record evidence. They
are excluded from equality only under semantic alignment, because equality of
local identifiers is not evidence of economic equality across engines.

## Boundary

V1.5F adds no tolerance, materiality threshold, pass/fail verdict, divergence
score, causal attribution, implementation-risk report, UI, CLI, persistence,
backtest execution, broker integration, or live-trading behavior. Those remain
later, separate decisions.

The committed `examples/v1_5f_comparison` files are regenerated by
`scripts/generate_v1_5f_comparison_example.py`; `--check` verifies byte-for-byte
determinism.
